Templates to create or adapt in class

Move the working Kubernetes structure into templates only after the plain
manifests have been proven. Start with one Deployment and Service, then extract
only the environment differences into values.yaml.

Suggested order:
1. _helpers.tpl
2. configmap.yaml
3. catalog.yaml
4. orders.yaml
5. storefront.yaml
6. ingress.yaml

Use the Session 5 rhythm: render, predict, mutate, prove.
